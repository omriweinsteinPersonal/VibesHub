The monochrome Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube, Facebook, and Pinterest SVGs are from
[Simple Icons v16](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons). The LinkedIn SVG is
from [Simple Icons v13.21.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/v13.21.0).
They are stored locally so storefront links do not depend on an external icon service
at runtime.
